# Roadmap

Milestones are outcome-based and intentionally have no dates. Move to the next milestone when the prior one has answered its main risk.

## 0. Product decisions and validation

- Choose the initial learner group, age range, language, and first device targets.
- Gather representative study pages with permission and identify handwriting/print quality expectations.
- Define a question/evidence contract and how learners review corrections.
- Compare OCR and generation options on a small, representative sample for quality, latency, privacy, and cost.

**Exit:** the target use case and a credible scan-to-useful-question path are specific enough to prototype.

## 1. Learning-loop prototype

- Create the Expo app shell and one end-to-end flow using sample or entered text.
- Generate a small evidence-backed question set, allow correction, and complete a short quiz.
- Save material and attempts locally; add a simple due-review state.
- Reward completion with a minimal creature collection interaction.

**Exit:** learners can complete the full loop and give meaningful feedback on question quality and motivation.

## 2. Capture and OCR

- Add camera capture and OCR for the agreed initial source type.
- Add text correction and clear failure/retry states.
- Re-evaluate time-to-first-session, OCR correction burden, and provider costs.

**Exit:** capture is sufficiently reliable for the selected audience and material type.

## 3. Retention and polish

- Improve review timing and question variation using observed answer history.
- Add onboarding, accessibility, persistence/error handling, and measured game progression.
- Revisit sign-in/sync only if user needs justify the added data and operating model.

**Exit:** observed repeat study supports continuing investment in the product.

## Later experiments

Battle mechanics, larger creature progression, step energy, background pedometer support, PDF import, teacher features, accounts/sync, and social play are possible experiments, not committed milestones.
